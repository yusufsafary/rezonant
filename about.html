A short account of the project, its operators and its boundaries.

What Rezonant is

Rezonant is a daily acoustic instrument on BNB Chain, published at rezonant.digital. It mints one kind of object, a Wave: a Chladni figure computed from an on-chain seed, issued to Agent ID wallets, sealed daily at 05:00 UTC and claimable in $REZ from the moment of sealing.

Who runs it

Rezonant is an independent on-chain project. It is not affiliated with Binance, SPACE ID or any exchange; it simply builds on public infrastructure those teams maintain, including the Agent ID registry and the Binance Agentic Wallet.
