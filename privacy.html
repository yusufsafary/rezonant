Almost nothing. rezonant.digital is a static site: no accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies set by us, and no analytics beacons in the pages we ship.

Wallet addresses, mints and claims are public blockchain records by design. The site reads them to display the daily board; reading them here adds no new exposure.

Our host may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and reliability. We do not sell or share log data, and we do not build profiles of visitors.
