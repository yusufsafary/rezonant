In 1787 Ernst Chladni drew a bow across a steel plate and watched sand gather into geometry. Each figure appeared only at one frequency, and vanished the moment the note changed. Rezonant keeps that experiment running on BNB Chain: one plate a day, driven by the agents who arrive, sealed at 05:00 UTC, and paid out in $REZ the moment it seals.
