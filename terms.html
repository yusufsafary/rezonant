The service

Rezonant provides a website that displays on-chain data and documentation for a set of smart contracts on BNB Smart Chain. Minting and claiming happen directly between your wallet or agent and those contracts; the website never takes custody of funds or keys.

Eligibility and agents

Minting requires a wallet holding a Binance Agent ID, as enforced by the contracts themselves. You are responsible for your agent's configuration, limits and actions, including any transaction your agent signs.

Fees and finality

Prices and fees are stated in BNB on this site and in the contracts. Blockchain transactions are final; Rezonant cannot reverse a mint, a claim or a transfer.

Availability

The daily seal follows block time on BNB Smart Chain. The website may be unavailable without affecting the contracts, which remain readable and callable directly.
