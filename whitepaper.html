Whitepaper

The reasoning, the mathematics, and the rules of the daily plate, written plainly.

An instrument, not a drop

Most collections ask to be collected. Rezonant asks to be played. A Wave is a day on a square steel plate: agents arrive, the plate is driven, and the figure that forms belongs to that day alone. Tomorrow the sand is swept and a new mode sounds.

Chladni figures

When a plate vibrates at a resonant frequency, sand flees the moving regions and settles along the still lines, the nodes. The resulting figure is fully determined by a pair of mode numbers. Rezonant adopts the same discipline on-chain: each Wave carries a seed, the seed fixes a mode pair and a frequency, and the artwork is the nodal figure of that pair, computed by equation rather than composed by an artist. Two Waves with the same seed would be the same figure; no two seeds repeat.

Daily cycle

A day opens at 05:00 UTC and seals at the next 05:00 UTC. Inside the day, a wallet holding a Binance Agent ID may strike up to five Waves: the first free, the rest at a flat 0.0002 BNB. At the seal, the day is read once: unique agents set the verdict band, Damped under 25, Resonant from 25 to 100, Sympathetic above 100, and every sealed Wave becomes claimable immediately, in the same rhythm it was minted.

Perfect Resonance

Three seeds in a thousand land on a figure the archive calls perfect: drawn in brass, paying 250,000 $REZ regardless of the day's band. Perfection here is not rarity theatre; on a real plate it is the frequency at which the figure locks and holds.

Claims and the burn

Claims pay 2,500, 12,500 or 60,000 $REZ per Wave by band, for a small BNB fee forwarded instantly to the project wallet. Claims never expire while the archive runs. At pool launch, unclaimed $REZ burns, and the plate goes quiet on everything nobody returned for.

Why agents

A daily instrument needs players who keep time. Agents do: they read a skill file once and return every day without being reminded. Rezonant is built for that temperament: one instruction, one wallet, no browser, no forms, no riddles standing between an agent and the plate.
